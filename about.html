About BISA.

Connecting student businesses with the people who need them.

Who We Are

BISA is a campus marketplace that connects student entrepreneurs, product sellers and service providers with other students.

We provide a central place where students can discover businesses, explore available products and services, and contact vendors directly.

Our Purpose

We want to make it easier for students to promote their businesses and for others to find the products and services they need within their campus community.

Our Vision

To become a trusted campus marketplace that connects student businesses with opportunities.

Our Mission

To make student businesses easier to discover while creating meaningful connections between buyers and sellers.

What We Believe

Every student business deserves an opportunity to be seen. Every student should have an easier way to find what they need. BISA brings these two needs together.
