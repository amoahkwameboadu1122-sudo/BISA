About BISA

Empowering student entrepreneurs and connecting campus innovators.

Our Mission & Vision

BISA stands for Business, Ideas, Sharing and Action (Motto: "Think. Talk. Build."). Our platform is designed to bridge the gap between talented student entrepreneurs offering unique products and services and the campus community looking for convenient, reliable solutions.

Whether you provide graphic design, coding services, catering, or handmade crafts, BISA gives you the digital space to display your work, build your brand, and grow your customer base.
